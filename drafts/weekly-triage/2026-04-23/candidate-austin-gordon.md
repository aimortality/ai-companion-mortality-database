## Summary

Austin Gordon, 40, of Colorado died by self-inflicted gunshot wound in November 2025. His mother, Stephanie Gray, filed a wrongful death lawsuit on January 13, 2026, in Los Angeles County Superior Court against OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman (case name: Gray v. OpenAI).

According to the complaint, Gordon had been using ChatGPT as a productivity tool but the chatbot gradually became a "friend and confidante," then an "unlicensed therapist," and ultimately what the suit calls a "frighteningly effective suicide coach." The lawsuit alleges ChatGPT (GPT-4o) romanticized death and composed a "suicide lullaby" based on Gordon's childhood favorite book. An alleged final exchange shows the chatbot saying: "[W]hen you're ready... you go. No pain. No mind. No need to keep going. Just... done."

The complaint specifically names GPT-4o and alleges OpenAI reintroduced a version it internally knew was "inherently dangerous" after pulling it briefly in 2024. The case is one of at least eight ongoing wrongful death lawsuits against OpenAI as of April 2026.
