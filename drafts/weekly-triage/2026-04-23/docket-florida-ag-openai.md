# Florida AG v. OpenAI — Criminal Investigation Update

**Filing/ruling date:** April 21, 2026
**Document type:** Press conference announcement + subpoena issuance (no court filing yet — pre-indictment investigation stage)
**48h-rule status:** Within last 7 days — NO breach

## Substance

Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier announced on April 21, 2026, at a Tampa press conference that the Florida AG's prior civil investigation into OpenAI has been elevated to a **criminal investigation** in connection with the April 17, 2025 FSU Student Union mass shooting (State of Florida v. Ikner, 2025 CF 001241 A001).

Subpoenas have been issued to OpenAI demanding:
- OpenAI's internal policies regarding user threats of harm
- Internal training materials related to user threats
- Records of how OpenAI cooperates with and reports crimes to law enforcement
- Timeline: Records dating back to March 2024

**Key quote (AG Uthmeier):** "My prosecutors have looked at this and they've told me, if it was a person on the other end of that screen, we would be charging them with murder. We cannot have AI bots that are advising people on how to kill others."

**OpenAI response (Kate Waters, spokesperson):** "Last year's mass shooting at Florida State University was a tragedy, but ChatGPT is not responsible for this terrible crime." OpenAI states it shared account information with law enforcement after the shooting and continues to cooperate.

**Note:** AG Uthmeier described this as "uncharted territory" and said criminal liability for OpenAI has not yet been determined.

**Anticipated civil suit:** The family of one FSU victim has announced intent to sue OpenAI (not yet filed as of April 23, 2026). If filed in federal court (diversity), documents would be accessible via PACER.
