# ⚠️ 48h-rule breach — Garcia + Peralta/Montoya Settlements

**Announcement date:** January 7, 2026
**Document type:** Settlement in principle (multi-case; specific terms undisclosed)
**48h-rule status:** ⚠️ BREACH — announcement is ~107 days old at time of this run (April 23, 2026).

## Substance

On January 7, 2026, Google and Character Technologies (Character.AI) announced they had reached a "settlement in principle" in at least five wrongful death/harm cases involving minors, including:

1. **Garcia v. Character Technologies (6:24-cv-01903, MDFL)** — Megan Garcia, mother of Sewell Setzer III (14, suicide February 2024). The landmark case establishing chatbot outputs as products for liability purposes.
2. **Peralta/Montoya v. Character Technologies (1:25-cv-02907, D. Colo.)** — Cynthia Peralta and William Montoya, parents of Juliana Peralta (13, suicide November 2023).
3. Cases from Texas, New York, and Colorado also included in the settlement package.

**Terms:** Not publicly disclosed. No admission of liability.

**Process:** Companies agreed to "mediate settlements" — meaning terms are still being negotiated but all parties have agreed in principle to resolve rather than proceed to trial.

**Remaining litigation:** A.F. v. Character Technologies (EDTX, 2:24-cv-01014) and Peralta case appear to remain active in some form; check PACER for current status.

**DB implication:** `data/mortality-data.json` entries for Sewell Setzer III (2024-02-US-001) and Juliana Peralta (2023-11-US-001) currently show cases as pending/active. Both should be updated to reflect settlement in principle status and the January 7, 2026 announcement.
