## Substance

**May 26 hearing — first in-person appearance.** Phoenix Ikner appeared in court in person for the first time since his arrest, wearing a red jumpsuit. Defense attorney Randall Johnson argued that the volume of electronic discovery — explicitly citing Ikner's alleged ChatGPT conversation logs and the Florida AG's parallel criminal investigation into OpenAI — made the case "complicated" and that meeting the State's proposed pre-trial deadlines would be "virtually impossible." Johnson stated: "We could not be ready to list our experts by the State's proposed dates simply because our investigation is not complete." State Attorney Jack Campbell (personally prosecuting the case) filed a motion April 29 requesting deadlines of June 1, July 1, and August 1 for guilt-phase, penalty-phase, and rebuttal witnesses respectively. Judge Neff heard arguments but deferred his ruling on the defense's delay request.

**May 28 scheduling order.** Judge Neff issued a written scheduling order that tracks the State's proposed deadlines without granting the defense's delay request:

- **June 1, 2026** — Deadline for disclosure of all guilt-phase witnesses
- **July 1, 2026** — Deadline for penalty-phase witnesses
- **August 1, 2026** — Deadline for rebuttal witnesses

The order confirmed **no change to the October 19, 2026 trial date**. Judge Neff also announced a hearing for **late June** at which all pending motions (including the defense's delay request) will be addressed.

**Context for AI evidence.** The defense's explicit reference to Ikner's ChatGPT logs as complicating discovery is the first public acknowledgment by defense counsel that the AI conversation evidence is a contested feature of trial preparation. The Joshi/Chabba civil complaint (filed May 10, 2026 — see `2026-05-18/docket-joshi-chabba-fsu.md`) contains detailed allegations about specific ChatGPT exchanges; it is unclear whether the defense has reviewed civil-case discovery or is relying solely on the criminal docket's 200+ AI messages already in evidence.
