## Substance

**Background recap:** Kim So-young was arrested February 11, 2026 after Seoul Gangbuk Police forensically extracted her ChatGPT search history. She had queried ChatGPT about lethal sleeping-pill doses before killing two men (January 28 and February 9, 2026) and seriously injuring a third (December 2025). Prosecutors used the ChatGPT logs as premeditation evidence to upgrade charges from inflicting-bodily-injury-resulting-in-death to murder. See `data/mortality-data.json` incident `2026-01-KR-001` for full case record.

**First hearing (April 9):** Kim denied both murder charges. Defense counsel acknowledged she gave the beverages but contested that there was a "foreseeable possibility" of death. Victims' families attended and called for the death penalty.

**IQ defense (April 15):** Korea Times reported that doubts about Kim's cognitive ability (low IQ) were raised by the defense to challenge the required mens rea for murder.

**Additional charges (April 30):** Charges for the December 2025 assault (first victim, who survived) were formally added to the indictment.

**Second hearing (May 7):** Witness questioning was converted to closed-door (비공개) proceedings, suggesting sensitive testimony — likely involving the surviving December victim or forensic expert witnesses regarding the ChatGPT evidence.

**Upcoming third hearing (June 11):** The third hearing is scheduled for June 11, 2026 at 4:30 PM Seoul time at the Seoul Northern District Court. No reporting on its expected content is available as of June 8.
