## Substance

A Florida federal judge (Judge Conway) rejected a motion by an Orlando law firm seeking a fee allocation from the Character.AI / Google multi-case settlement announced January 7, 2026. Per the available search summary, the judge characterized the firm's supporting statement as "demonstrably untrue."

This is a collateral fee dispute, not a ruling on the merits of the underlying wrongful death claims. The January 7, 2026 settlement itself (Garcia, Peralta/Montoya, plus 3+ additional Character.AI cases) was confidential; no admission of liability. The settlement appears to have been finalized within the 90-day window specified in the January announcement.

**Relevance to database:** This ruling indicates the settlement process is substantially complete at the court level, with collateral disputes now being the primary pending matter rather than the underlying claims.
