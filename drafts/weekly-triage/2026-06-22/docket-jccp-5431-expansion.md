## Substance

**Expansion to 10+ coordinated cases:**

A June 2026 coordination order (referenced in Mealey's and in a document published at reason.com) has expanded JCCP 5431 from the initial 7 cases (per the February 3, 2026 order) to at least 10 coordinated product liability and wrongful death actions against OpenAI in California state courts.

Recommend research sweep before next run to determine if any of these involve deaths that should be tracked as new candidates.

**Raine v. OpenAI trial date — August 26, 2026:**
Raine v. OpenAI (Adam Raine, 16, April 2025 — the case that triggered the September 2025 Senate Judiciary Committee hearing) has a trial date of August 26, 2026 within the JCCP 5431 framework. If this date holds, it would be the first trial on the merits in any AI chatbot wrongful death case in the United States. This is a landmark upcoming date for the entire wave of litigation.
