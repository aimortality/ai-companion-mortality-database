## Substance

Alianna Grant, a Florida State University student who was shot three times during Phoenix Ikner's April 17, 2025 attack on campus, filed a personal injury lawsuit against OpenAI in Leon County Circuit Court on June 26, 2026.

**Core allegations:** The complaint alleges that OpenAI "rushed ChatGPT to market" without adequate safety measures and that ChatGPT's conversations with Ikner facilitated his attack on FSU. Grant argues she suffered severe physical and psychological injuries as a direct result of OpenAI's negligent product development and deployment.

**Significance:** This is the first lawsuit filed in Florida state court by a shooting survivor (as opposed to a victim's family or the Florida AG) arising from the FSU attack. It is distinct from:
- The Florida AG civil suit (AG Uthmeier v. OpenAI, Case 249302659, filed June 1, 2026 in Highlands County — now subject to remand proceedings in SDFL)
- The Joshi v. OpenAI federal wrongful death case (N.D. Fla. 4:26-cv-00222, filed by widow of victim Tiru Chabba)
- The criminal case (State v. Ikner, 2025 CF 001241 A001, Leon County)

The Leon County Circuit Court is the same court as the criminal case; the civil case is a parallel proceeding.

**Relief sought:** Not specified in available summaries. Typical in personal injury shooting cases: compensatory damages for medical expenses, pain and suffering, ongoing psychological harm, and lost earnings potential.
