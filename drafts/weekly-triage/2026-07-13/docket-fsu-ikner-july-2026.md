## Substance

**Late-June motions hearing (est. June 25–26, 2026):** Judge Lance Neff held a motions hearing in late June 2026 to address all pending pretrial motions, including the defense's request to delay the October 19 trial. Defense counsel argued the "overwhelming volume" of discovery — including approximately 800+ GB of video footage, 239 9-1-1 recordings, 120+ Tallahassee PD reports, and thousands of pages of ChatGPT conversation logs — made adequate preparation impossible under the current timeline.

Judge Neff denied the delay request and kept the October 19, 2026 trial date over defense objections. Per reporting on the hearing, Neff stated: "You get a fair trial, not a perfect one."

**July 1 penalty-phase witness disclosure deadline:** The May 28, 2026 scheduling order set July 1 as the deadline for state disclosure of guilt-phase witnesses. That deadline passed during this triage window (July 6–13) with no reported default or extension request. The state appears to have met the deadline.

**ChatGPT evidence:** The defendant's ChatGPT conversation logs remain central to both the prosecution and the defense. Prosecution evidence (as reported in multiple Nexstar affiliates' coverage of the late-June hearing) includes logs in which Ikner: researched "how would the country react if there was a shooting at FSU," queried how to select ammunition to maximize casualties, and used ChatGPT to plan tactical elements of the attack. Defense is relying in part on the volume of this digital discovery as grounds for a continuance.
