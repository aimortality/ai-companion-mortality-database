## Substance

**Third hearing (~June 11–12):** At the third trial hearing, Kim So-young's defense counsel denied the assault charges relating to the three additional victims indicted April 30. Defense stated: "No drugged drinks were given [to these victims]." This is a narrower denial than at the first hearing (April 9, where defense denied the murder charges but acknowledged Kim gave beverages). The June hearing's additional victim denial suggests the defense is now contesting the factual predicate of the assault charges while maintaining the prior position on the murder charges.

The third hearing was closed to the public in part; Chief Judge Oh Byung-hee is presiding over the 14th Criminal Division.

**Fourth hearing (July 14):** The fourth hearing is scheduled for 2:30 PM Seoul time on July 14, 2026 — one day after this triage run. It is expected to include witness examination of victims (비공개 examination of some witnesses may continue). No reporting on expected content is available as of July 13.

**Background (from database entry `2026-01-KR-001`):** Kim So-young was arrested February 11, 2026 after Seoul Gangbuk Police forensically extracted ChatGPT search history from her devices. She had queried ChatGPT for lethal sleeping-pill doses before killing two men (January 28 and February 9, 2026) and seriously injuring a third (December 2025). Prosecutors used the ChatGPT search logs as premeditation evidence to upgrade charges from injury-resulting-in-death to murder.
