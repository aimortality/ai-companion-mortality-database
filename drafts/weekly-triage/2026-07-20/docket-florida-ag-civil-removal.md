# Docket Update: State of Florida v. OpenAI — Civil Lawsuit Removal/Remand Fight

**Case:** State of Florida (AG James Uthmeier) v. OpenAI Inc. et al.  
**Court:** Originally filed: Leon County Circuit Court, Florida. Removed to: U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Florida (Tallahassee Division), assigned to Judge Aileen Cannon (visiting from S.D. Fla.).  
**Filed:** Civil lawsuit filed June 1, 2026 (Leon County Circuit). Notice of removal filed July 2, 2026.  
**Related cases:** Florida v. Ikner (2025 CF 001241 A001, Leon Co. Circuit Court — criminal); case is derivative of the FSU mass shooting (April 17, 2025, Tallahassee) and the Adam Raine relational-pathway case.

---

## Filing / ruling date

- **June 1, 2026** — Florida AG James Uthmeier files State of Florida v. OpenAI in Leon County Circuit Court. Florida is the first U.S. state to file a civil lawsuit against OpenAI related to chatbot-linked deaths. The complaint focuses on ChatGPT's role in the FSU mass shooting (via shooter Phoenix Ikner's reliance on ChatGPT) and the Adam Raine suicide case (relational-pathway, already in the database as 2024-US-004).
- **July 2, 2026** — OpenAI files a notice of removal to federal court (N.D. Fla., Tallahassee), asserting federal-question jurisdiction. The removal is filed 31 days after the complaint.
- **July 10, 2026** — Florida AG files a motion to remand the case back to Leon County Circuit Court, arguing that OpenAI's federal-question basis is insufficient and that state consumer protection and public nuisance theories should be adjudicated in state court.

---

## Substance

The Florida civil complaint alleges that OpenAI's ChatGPT platform constituted a public nuisance, violated Florida's Deceptive and Unfair Trade Practices Act, and negligently designed a product that enabled the deaths tracked in the database. The removal/remand fight centers on whether Section 230 preemption and other federal defenses create sufficient federal-question jurisdiction to pull the case to federal court.

Judge Aileen Cannon (of S.D. Fla., sitting by designation in N.D. Fla.) has received the case on the federal docket. No scheduling order has been entered as of the available reporting.

---

## Significance for database

This is the first state-government civil action against OpenAI over chatbot-linked deaths in the database. If remanded to state court, it would create a parallel Leon County proceeding alongside the existing Ikner criminal case (same courthouse). If it remains in federal court before Judge Cannon, it creates a potential appellate pathway distinct from the SF Superior Court JCCP 5431 coordination.
