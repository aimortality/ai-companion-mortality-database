## Substance

Google's MTD (filed earlier, opposition filed by plaintiffs, now Google reply brief July 15) argues:
- Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act bars the claims
- The claims fail to establish a plausible causal link between Gemini's conduct and Gavalas's death
- First Amendment concerns with holding AI companies liable for generated content

The July 15 reply brief represents Google's final argument before the August 19 oral hearing. The N.D. Cal. court's ruling on this MTD will be a landmark for AI chatbot liability: if Section 230 is applied to defeat the claims, it will significantly constrain the legal theories in all cases in the database (including the ChatGPT cases in JCCP 5431). If the court applies the B.L. v.
