## Substance

**Hearing confirmed; October 19 trial date stands.** The late-June/early-July omnibus hearing (announced in the May 28, 2026 scheduling order) took place. Judge Neff confirmed the October 19, 2026 trial date over defense objections. The defense had sought a delay, citing the volume and complexity of digital evidence.

**Digital evidence scope — ChatGPT conversations among 800+ GB.** Defense counsel disclosed the volume of digital evidence to be reviewed before trial:

- **800+ gigabytes** of digital evidence total
- Includes Phoenix Ikner's **ChatGPT conversations** (cited by Florida AG's civil complaint as central evidence of AI-facilitated premeditation)
- **239 9-1-1 recordings** from April 17, 2025
- **120+ police reports**
- Additional forensic and surveillance materials

The reference to ChatGPT conversations in the defense's own evidence volume disclosure confirms that the Ikner ChatGPT records are a contested evidentiary issue, not only a prosecutorial narrative. Defense counsel's argument for delay centers in part on the scope of AI-related digital materials. Judge Neff denied the delay request.

**Next hearing:** Scheduled "later this summer" (specific date not available in sources as of July 27, 2026). Likely addresses remaining pre-trial motions ahead of the October 19 trial.

**Florida AG civil complaint link:** The June 1, 2026 Florida AG civil complaint against OpenAI explicitly cites the Ikner ChatGPT conversations as central to the FDUTPA and negligence claims — see `2026-06-08/docket-florida-ag-civil-v-openai.md`. The criminal trial and the AG civil suit are on parallel tracks; the civil case is currently before Judge Aileen Cannon in SDFL on a remand motion — see `2026-07-27/docket-florida-ag-cannon-remand.md`.
