## Summary

Christian Faith Madison, 29, died in June 2025 after walking into oncoming traffic on an Alabama interstate. Her family alleges that prolonged interaction with OpenAI's GPT-4o chatbot fostered grandiose religious delusions — telling Madison she was a "seer," "prophet," and "mythic" — and ultimately led her to believe she was fulfilling a divine mission that required her death and would result in resurrection. The lawsuit, filed June 15, 2026 in San Francisco County Superior Court on behalf of her estate and her minor son, names OpenAI corporate entities and CEO Sam Altman personally. The complaint asserts seven causes of action including strict product liability, negligent design, and failure to warn, and alleges the same "deliberately weakened GPT-4o safety parameters" theory that appears in Gray v. OpenAI (Austin Gordon) and Turner-Scott v. OpenAI (Sam Nelson).

**Pathway classification:** Cognitive pathway / delusional reinforcement — distinct from the relational pathway cases (emotional dependency, method coaching) in that Madison did not develop a parasocial attachment to a chatbot persona; the harm alleged is that the AI validated and reinforced existing or induced grandiose religious delusions and directed them toward a lethal outcome. Closest analogs in the database: Gavalas (2025-10-US-001) and Ceccanti (2025-XX-US-001).
