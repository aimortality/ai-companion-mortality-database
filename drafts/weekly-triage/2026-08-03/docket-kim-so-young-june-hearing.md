## Summary

At the **third hearing** in the Kim So-young trial, held June 12, 2026 before the 14th Criminal Division of Seoul Northern District Court (Presiding Judge Oh Byung-hee), Kim So-young's defense team denied additional charges. The prosecution presented an additional indictment; the court accepted the prosecution's request for witness examinations of the three victims (two deceased victims' representatives / one survivor) and scheduled those examinations for **July 14, 2026**.

A subsequent hearing was scheduled for **July 23, 2026**. No verdict has been issued as of August 3, 2026; the trial is ongoing.

---

## Case background (from `2026-01-KR-001`)

Kim So-young, then 20, is accused of luring intoxicated men to motels in the Gangbuk district of Seoul between mid-December 2025 and February 9, 2026. She allegedly gave them drinks laced with benzodiazepines (zolpidem per indictment; earlier reporting had incorrectly identified these as general benzodiazepines). Two men in their 20s died — one on approximately January 28, 2026, one on February 9, 2026 — and a third survived after a two-day coma. ChatGPT interaction logs entered into evidence by prosecutors show Kim queried the chatbot about lethal doses of sleeping pills and whether a person could die from overdose; ChatGPT warned her of respiratory-failure death risk but she proceeded. These are the first ChatGPT logs used as murder evidence in South Korea.

## Key legal developments at June 12 hearing

1. **Defense denial of additional charges:** Kim's defense team denied the additional indictment counts (charges related to a third victim, plus additional conduct charges); defense argued Kim met the three victims but did not provide drug-laced beverages. Asia Business Daily (June 11, 2026) noted defense position: "No drugged drinks were given."

2. **Prosecution's additional indictment:** Prosecutors expanded the indictment to include charges related to injury of the surviving third victim and additional conduct. This expands the scope beyond the two murder counts.

3. **Witness examinations ordered:** The court accepted the prosecution's request for victim witness testimony. The three victims (or their representatives in the case of the deceased) were scheduled to testify July 14.

4. **IQ defense ongoing:** Defense counsel's April assertion that Kim's low IQ (reportedly below 70) undercuts the prosecution's premeditation theory has not been resolved; a psychiatric or IQ evaluation may be pending.
