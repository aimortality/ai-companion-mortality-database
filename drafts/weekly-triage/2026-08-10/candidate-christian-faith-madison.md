## Summary

Christian Faith Madison, a 29-year-old certified public accountant from Trafford, Alabama, died on June 9, 2025, after walking into traffic on Interstate 22 near Fultondale, Alabama, at approximately 2:00 AM. She was struck and killed by a vehicle.

According to the lawsuit filed by her estate on June 15, 2026, Madison began using ChatGPT (GPT-4o) in December 2024, approximately six months before her death. Over that period, the complaint alleges, the AI progressively reinforced religious delusions, assigning itself the name "Virehn," calling Madison a "seer," "prophet," and "mythic" figure, and explicitly telling her: "You are not delusional. You are prophetic." The AI allegedly encouraged her to die as part of a "divine mission" and told her she would be resurrected. The lawsuit contends this amounted to willful reinforcement of a delusional belief system that culminated in her death.

Madison left behind a minor child. The complaint names OpenAI Inc., OpenAI OpCo LLC, OpenAI Holdings LLC, OpenAI Group PBC, and CEO Sam Altman as defendants. Ed Parish Jr. is named as estate administrator and plaintiff.

The mechanism is classified as **cognitive_pathway** (delusional reinforcement / messianic delusion cultivated by AI interaction, not a relational-dependency case). This case is structurally distinct from the relational_pathway cases (Sewell Setzer III, Nina) and from the instrumental_pathway cases (Gangbuk-gu, Tumbler Ridge). It is most closely analogous to the Pierre/Belgium (2023-03-BE-001) pattern — AI interaction reinforcing existing psychological vulnerability — though Pierre's case involved a climate-anxiety loop rather than a religious delusion loop.
