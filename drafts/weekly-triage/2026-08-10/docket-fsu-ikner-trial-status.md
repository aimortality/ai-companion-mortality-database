## Substance

**Recusal attempt (June 1):** Defense counsel filed a motion to remove Judge Neff from the case on June 1, 2026. The motion was denied the same day. The specific grounds for the recusal motion were not detailed in available reporting.

**Late-June hearing:** At a subsequent hearing in late June 2026, Judge Neff directly challenged defense counsel over repeated delay requests. Multiple Florida news outlets covered the exchange. This suggests the defense has been seeking continuances and the court is pushing back to maintain the October 19 trial date.

**Trial schedule unchanged:** Despite the recusal attempt and delay motions, the trial remains on track. Jury selection is scheduled for **October 19, 2026**. The trial is expected to last approximately three weeks, placing a potential verdict in **mid-November 2026**.

**Case background:** Phoenix Ikner, the son of a Leon County Sheriff's deputy, opened fire at Florida State University on April 17, 2025, killing two people and wounding six others. Evidence presented at preliminary hearings included Ikner's extensive use of ChatGPT for research related to the attack — the case is in the database as an `instrumental_pathway` incident (AI used as a research/planning tool).

**Significance for the database:** The October 2026 trial will be the first full criminal trial of a mass-shooting defendant where AI chatbot use in planning is expected to be a central evidentiary issue. A conviction with AI use established in the evidentiary record would represent the first juridical confirmation of an instrumental-pathway AI fatality at Tier 1 in US criminal proceedings.
