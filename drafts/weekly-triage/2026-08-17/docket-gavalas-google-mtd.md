## Substance

Google/Alphabet has filed a Rule 12(b)(6) motion to dismiss the Gavalas wrongful-death complaint. The motion argues failure to state a claim on which relief can be granted. The hearing is set for August 19, 2026.

This is the **first federal district court motion to dismiss in a Gemini wrongful-death case** and only the **second federal MTD in an AI chatbot wrongful-death case overall** — the first was Soelberg v. Character.AI (N.D. Cal.), where Judge Seeborg denied the MTD on April 13, 2026, allowing the case to proceed.

The case management conference (CMC) has been continued to September 16, 2026, likely to follow the MTD ruling.

**Legal significance:** If Judge Lee denies the MTD (consistent with Seeborg's Soelberg ruling), Gavalas v. Google proceeds to discovery and likely trial. If Lee grants the MTD, it is the first federal dismissal of an AI chatbot wrongful-death complaint — a significant divergence from the Soelberg precedent and a major development for the field.

**Section 230 watch:** It is not confirmed whether Google's MTD invokes Section 230 CDA immunity. If it does, the ruling will be the first federal ruling on Section 230 applicability to Gemini wrongful-death claims.
